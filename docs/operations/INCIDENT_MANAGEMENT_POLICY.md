# SPLINCI COMMERCE OS — INCIDENT MANAGEMENT POLICY

## 1. Severity Classification & Lifecycle
- **SEV_1:** Critical system outage.
- **SEV_2:** Major functional degradation.
- **SEV_3:** Minor functional defect.
- **SEV_4:** Low impact / cosmetic issue.
- Lifecycle: `OPEN` -> `INVESTIGATING` -> `IDENTIFIED` -> `MONITORING` -> `RESOLVED` -> `CLOSED`.
