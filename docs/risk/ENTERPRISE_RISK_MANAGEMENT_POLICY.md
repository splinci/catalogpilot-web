# SPLINCI COMMERCE OS — ENTERPRISE RISK MANAGEMENT POLICY

## 1. Risk Governance Taxonomy
- The Enterprise Risk Register (`ENTERPRISE_RISK_REGISTER`) tracks risks across Security, Availability, Financial, Compliance, Privacy, and AI Safety domains.
- Every enterprise risk must define an assigned C-level / VP owner, inherent risk score, residual risk score, mitigation treatment plan, and mapped GOV controls.
